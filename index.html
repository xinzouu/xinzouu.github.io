About

I am a mathematics Ph.D. student at Johns Hopkins University, working with Yiannis Sakellaridis. My research lies at the intersection of number theory, representation theory, and harmonic analysis, motivated by the Langlands program.

I study periods and L-functions, with particular interests in archimedean zeta integrals, harmonic analysis on spherical varieties, representations of real groups, special L-values, and period relations.
